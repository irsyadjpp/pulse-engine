# 6. Data Retention Policy

## 6.1 Retention Schedule

| Data Type | Retention Period | Legal Basis | Disposal Method |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Customer PII | 10 years after policy expiry | OJK regulation | Secure deletion |
| Policy Data | 10 years after policy expiry | OJK regulation | Archival then deletion |
| Audit Trail | 7 years | UU PDP, OJK | Archival then deletion |
| Transaction Log | 7 years | Tax regulation | Archival then deletion |
| Communication Log | 5 years | Company policy | Secure deletion |
| Consent Records | 10 years | UU PDP | Archival then deletion |

## 6.2 Disposal Procedures

### Secure Deletion

* Use secure deletion tools (shredding)
* Verify deletion completion
* Document disposal
* Update retention register

### Archival

* Compress data
* Encrypt archival data
* Store in secure location
* Document archival
